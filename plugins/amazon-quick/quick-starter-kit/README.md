# Quick Starter Kit

Two everyday writing helpers that get you productive in Amazon Quick on day one.
Install once and both skills are ready to use in chat.

## What's included

- **Meeting Notes Summarizer** — paste raw notes or a transcript and get back a
  tight summary: a TL;DR, the decisions, an action-item table with owners and
  due dates, and any open questions.
- **Weekly Status Writer** — hand it a brain-dump of your week and get a clean
  update organized into Done, In progress, Blocked, and Next — ready to send.

## How to use it

After installing, just ask in chat — for example:

- "Summarize these meeting notes and pull out the action items."
- "Draft my weekly status update from these bullets."
