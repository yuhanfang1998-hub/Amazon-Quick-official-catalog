# Weekly Status Writer

Draft a weekly status update your manager or team can skim.

## What it produces

A short update in four sections:

- **Done** — what shipped or completed this week, each as a one-line result.
- **In progress** — what is underway, with a rough state (on track / at risk).
- **Blocked** — anything stuck, naming what it is waiting on.
- **Next** — the top one to three things planned for next week.

## How to use

Give it your raw material — bullet notes, a list of what you did, or a couple of
sentences — and it organizes them into the sections above. Lead each line with
the outcome, not the activity ("cut p99 latency 30%", not "worked on latency").
Keep it to what the source supports: do not invent progress, metrics, or dates
that were not provided. If a section has nothing, omit it rather than filling it
with filler.
